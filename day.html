Basílica de la Sagrada FamíliaBasilica

Under construction since 1882. The exterior is the famous part; the interior is the reason to go — a stone forest where the columns branch overhead and the glass throws red light west and blue east. Ninety minutes minimum.

Where you'll be

Jardins de Santa ClotildeGardens

The best thing near the hotel by a wide margin, and essentially next door — laid out along the clifftop in 1919 by Nicolau Rubió i Tudurí in the manner of an Italian Renaissance garden. Cypress avenues, statuary, ponds, and long staircases dropping through the pines to frame the sea. €6.10, open 10:00 to 20:00.

Maps ↗Navigate ▸Gardens Opens 10:00

Platja de Santa CristinaBeach

The hotel's own beach, straight down through the grounds — and the busy one of the two. It has a bar and restaurant, sunloungers, an events terrace, and its own paid car park off the hermitage road, so it draws day visitors from Lloret as well as the hotel. Lovely, and about ninety seconds from your room, but it is not a quiet cove. It is the reason the walk to Boadella is worth making.

Maps ↗Navigate ▸At the hotel The busy one

Cala Sa BoadellaBeach

The next cove north and the one to aim for — 250 m of sand with pines running down to it, no road, no buildings and a single small chiringuito. Getting there is the good part: up through the Santa Clotilde gardens and along the hermitage road, then a 200 m dirt path down through the trees. Fifteen to twenty minutes at a stroll, shaded most of the way, with the coast opening up as you drop. Note that Lloret's own beach listing describes Boadella as a traditionally nudist beach rather than one with a naturist corner — worth knowing before you commit the morning to it.

Where you'll be

Dalí built it inside the bombed-out shell of the town theatre and designed every room as a single work — the Mae West face you assemble by standing in the right spot, the Rainy Taxi in the courtyard, giant eggs along the roofline. Where you'll be

MontserratMonastery

A Benedictine monastery wedged into a mountain of pink conglomerate that erodes into hundreds of rounded fingers, visible from half of Catalonia. The Black Madonna is inside; the boys' choir sings most days around 1pm. The real draw is above the abbey — funiculars and ridge paths with a thousand-metre drop to the plain.
