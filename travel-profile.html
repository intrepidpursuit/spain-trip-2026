Travel Style

Slow pace — one or two activities per day, flexible time, no rushing. Will make exceptions for truly unmissable experiences but avoids typical tourist traps. Prefers off-the-beaten-path experiences, local food, and historic depth over surface-level sightseeing. Flexible on trip length — willing to extend for worthwhile experiences.

Interests

  • Museums — aerospace and contemporary art preferred, open to others
  • Historic sites — especially architecture and ruins
  • Local food — authentic, non-touristy
  • Live music, theater, and comedy
  • Nudist/naturist-friendly locations and beaches

Travel Companions

Wife: Melissa White. Does not work, flexible schedule. Not a nudist but accommodating for short periods. Shares interest in slow travel, food, and historic sites.

Dogs: Two dachshunds, 12-15 lbs each. Regularly travel on road trips with the dogs. Open to flying with them. Delta does not allow in-cabin pets on international flights — must board or use another carrier internationally.

Loyalty Programs

  • Delta SkyMiles: #9412645773 — Platinum Medallion
  • Delta Reserve Amex: Grants Centurion Lounge access when flying Delta (guest policy tightened mid-2026: guests must be on same Delta flight)
  • Hilton Honors: #539177017 — Diamond
  • National Car Rental: #344538670 — Executive Elite. Negotiated corporate rate through Meow Wolf.

Flight Preferences

  • Delta exclusively
  • Direct/nonstop only
  • Later departures preferred (not a morning person)
  • Books one-way individually ticketed flights for flexibility
  • Open to multicity routing if savings are meaningful and endpoints are fixed
  • Willing to drive ~1 hour from a better-served airport (e.g., ABQ over SAF for Santa Fe trips)

Hotel Preferences

  • King bed, always, when travelling with Melissa. A queen is acceptable only if there is no alternative and counts as a real downside — worth paying to avoid or switching hotels over. Twin or double-double is not acceptable for the two of them. Travelling solo, no bed preference.
  • Hilton Honors default (Diamond status, member #539177017)
  • IHG, Hyatt and Marriott are acceptable alternatives when Hilton has no good option
  • Wants a comfortable, well-appointed room; on-property amenities are close to irrelevant. Reference points: Home2 Suites, Hampton Inn. Not looking for luxury properties.
  • Travelling with friends, the default is the same hotel as the group unless another option is materially better
  • Amex Platinum — Fine Hotels + Resorts is available, but FHR books as a third party and earns no Honors points or elite credit, so book direct whenever points matter
  • Some frustration with Hilton upgrade practices vs. Hyatt Globalist
  • LA trips may require negotiated-rate hotels

Driving and Car Rental

  • An International Driving Permit is required in Spain and several other countries — one for each driver, and it takes a trip to AAA, so flag it early
  • Happy with an EV where the daily distances suit it; every EV is an automatic, which also solves the manual-transmission problem in Europe
  • European rentals bill in strict 24-hour periods with only ~30–59 minutes of grace, unlike the US. Booked pickup and return times matter — check both ends against the itinerary before booking, not after.

Work and Schedule

Works remotely for Meow Wolf (HQ in Santa Fe, NM) on Mountain Time. Generous PTO. Melissa has a fully flexible schedule. Both willing to extend trips for worthwhile experiences.

Recurring Travel

  • Santa Fe / Albuquerque: Quarterly Meow Wolf HQ visits. Flies ATL to ABQ (direct Delta), drives ~1 hour to Santa Fe.
  • New York City: Periodic trips building toward a 2028 exhibition opening.
  • Los Angeles: Exhibition opening, no firm dates. Negotiated-rate hotels likely required.
