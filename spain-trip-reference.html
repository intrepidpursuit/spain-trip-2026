Itinerary by Phase

Phase 1 — Andalusia (Sep 14-19)

  • Sep 14: Arrive Malaga 4:15pm. Check in, dinner in old town. Picasso Museum and Alcazaba worth a morning.
  • Sep 15 (Anniversary): Dinner in Malaga or Seville. Book somewhere special in advance.
  • Sep 16: Beach day. Cantarrijan (naturist-friendly, Maro-Cerro Gordo Natural Park, near Almunecar) or Benalnatura (Benalmadena, intimate cove, gay-friendly).
  • Sep 17-18: Seville, 2 nights. Alcazar (alcazarsevilla.org/prepara-la-visita/ — book in advance), cathedral, authentic flamenco in Triana.
  • Sep 19: Granada. Alhambra (book weeks in advance, sells out). Stay in Albaicin neighborhood. Morocco starts from here.

Phase 2 — Morocco (Sep 20-22)

  • Sep 20: Drive to Tarifa, FRS ferry (45 min) to Tangier. Old medina, Kasbah, stay in a riad.
  • Sep 21: Chefchaouen — The Blue City, 2hrs inland. Optional: Volubilis (Roman ruins, 1st century AD).
  • Sep 22: Ferry back to Tarifa, fly to Barcelona (~1.5hrs). Join wedding group. Park Guell at 3:30pm.

Phase 3 — Barcelona and Wedding (Sep 22-27)

See confirmed bookings above. Wedding group activities Sep 22-24. Girona Sep 25. Wedding Sep 26. Post-wedding brunch Sep 27.

Phase 4 — Wide Open (Sep 28 - Oct 2)

  • Sep 28: Noon vineyard picnic at Clos de Basella, then Dali Theatre-Museum (Figueres, 45 min), overnight Cadaques.
  • Sep 29-30: Costa Brava. Playa Illa Roja (near Begur, naturist-friendly) or Cala Sa Boadella (Lloret de Mar, naturist-friendly cove).
  • Oct 1: Barcelona — Mar Bella beach (clothing-optional section, young cosmopolitan crowd, LGBTQ-friendly, Poblenou).
  • Oct 2: Barcelona final day. Gothic Quarter, Picasso Museum, live music.
  • Oct 3: Fly home. BCN to ATL, DL195, departs 11:00am. Airport by 8:30am.

Naturist-Friendly Beaches

  • Cantarrijan — near Almunecar, Andalusia. Dramatic cliffs, crystal water, naturist section with beach bar. Mixed crowd all ages.
  • Benalnatura — Benalmadena. Intimate cove, gay-friendly, arrive early on weekdays.
  • Playa Illa Roja — near Begur, Costa Brava. Known naturist beach, wooded path access, peaceful.
  • Cala Sa Boadella — Lloret de Mar. Hidden naturist cove behind pine forest.
  • Mar Bella — Barcelona (Poblenou). Clothing-optional section, young cosmopolitan crowd, LGBTQ-friendly, 15 min from center.
  • Sitges — 30 min by train from Barcelona. Multiple clothing-optional beaches, younger crowd.
